# TechSavvy Gear design direction

- Identity: TechSavvy Gear, a merchandise extension of TechSavvy LLC.
- Mission: People solve tech. People matter more.
- Palette: #090B09 background, #111510 panels, #72FF2A accent, #F3F4ED text, #A3AAA0 secondary text.
- Typography: Barlow Condensed bold for display headlines; Inter for interface and body. Hosted locally by Next.js font optimization.
- Shape: square corners, restrained borders, generous spacing, large apparel-led hero.
- Imagery: original user-supplied brand art and storefront concept. Concept labels remain visible. No generated testimonials, customer counts, shipping promises, or claims of approved samples.
- Navigation: collection, story, and partnership are primary. Services link back to techsavvytechs.com.
- Responsive: four-column product grid on desktop, two columns below 800px. Mobile hero repositions imagery below the main message. Native cart dialog supports Escape and modal keyboard focus.
- Accessibility: skip link, semantic headings, labeled fields, pressed-state variant selectors, visible focus, reduced-motion support. This implementation has browser checks; it is not a certified WCAG audit.

Before a paid launch, approve physical samples and replace all concept crops with dedicated product and lifestyle images. Small artwork text in the supplied raster is not reliable production artwork.
