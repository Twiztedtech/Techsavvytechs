## What TechSavvy does

TechSavvy LLC is a low-voltage cabling, network infrastructure, managed IT (MSP), and RF/cell signal solutions company. Core service lines:

- **Low-voltage cabling** — certified Cat6/Cat6A and multi-mode fiber installs, precision rack dressing and labeling
- **Network infrastructure** — network architecture design, SD-WAN deployment, security appliance configuration
- **Managed IT (MSP)** — proactive monitoring, remote firmware management, security auditing
- **RF / cell signal** — a newer service line (cell signal boosters/coverage solutions)

## Who the customers actually are

TechSavvy doesn't fit neatly into one customer type — the business runs across several channels at once:

- **Direct NorCal clients** — businesses in Fairfield, Sacramento, and the South Bay/Bay Area who book a site survey/audit directly through the website.
- **Gig-platform work** — TechSavvy sources projects through WorkMarket and Field Nation, which surface companies nationwide needing new site builds and technology rollouts.
- **Technician staffing/subcontracting** — TechSavvy has supplied technicians for out-of-state businesses on short-term projects, acting as boots-on-the-ground labor for someone else's rollout.
- **MSP-to-MSP work** — some of TechSavvy's own customers are other MSPs, who bring TechSavvy in as a subcontractor/fulfillment partner rather than being the end client themselves.

Net effect: TechSavvy is as much a flexible field-services/fulfillment partner as it is a direct-to-business MSP. Don't assume a single "ideal customer profile" — the pitch and tone should flex depending on whether the audience is an end-business, an MSP partner, or a platform-sourced project.
